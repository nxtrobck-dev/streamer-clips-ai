Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

1. Information we collect

Streamer Clips AI does not require users to provide personal information directly to the developer. When users connect their TikTok account, TikTok may provide basic account information and authorization tokens.

2. How information is used

Information is used only to connect the user's TikTok account and enable video uploads requested by the user.

3. Data storage

Livestream clips are processed and stored locally on the user's computer. We do not operate a central server for storing user videos.

4. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. TikTok processes information according to its own privacy policy.

5. Security

Users should keep their account credentials and authorization tokens secure.

6. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the GitHub repository.
